    Woodford Reserve Bourbon — a whiskey from Woodford Reserve — comes in a 375 mL half bottle. Whiskey is distilled from fermented grain and aged in wooden casks, which typically gives it notes of vanilla, caramel, oak and spice; bourbon, rye, Irish and Scotch styles each put their own spin on that profile. Bourbon is an American whiskey made from at least 51% corn and aged in new charred oak barrels, which typically gives it a sweeter vanilla-and-caramel character.
