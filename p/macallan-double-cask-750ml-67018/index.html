    About this item

    Macallan Double Cask is a whiskey from Macallan, available at Convenience Liquors in a standard 750 mL bottle. Whiskey is distilled from fermented grain and aged in wooden casks, which typically gives it notes of vanilla, caramel, oak and spice; bourbon, rye, Irish and Scotch styles each put their own spin on that profile. Scotch whisky is made in Scotland and aged at least three years in oak; single malts and blends range from light and honeyed to rich and smoky. Enjoy it neat, with a splash of water, on a large ice cube, or in classics like the Old Fashioned, Manhattan or Whiskey Sour.
