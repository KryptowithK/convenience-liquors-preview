    About this item

    Bulleit Bourbon is a whiskey from Bulleit, available at Convenience Liquors in a 375 mL half bottle. Whiskey is distilled from fermented grain and aged in wooden casks, which typically gives it notes of vanilla, caramel, oak and spice; bourbon, rye, Irish and Scotch styles each put their own spin on that profile. Bourbon is an American whiskey made from at least 51% corn and aged in new charred oak barrels, which typically gives it a sweeter vanilla-and-caramel character. Enjoy it neat, with a splash of water, on a large ice cube, or in classics like the Old Fashioned, Manhattan or Whiskey Sour.
